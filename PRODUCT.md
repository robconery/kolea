# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Working developers reading Big Machine deliberately: they clicked through from the
newsletter or a share, sat down with coffee, and intend to read 1,500 words properly,
usually on a laptop in daylight. They are not skimming a feed.

## Product Purpose

The public reading site for Rob Conery's essays, served by Kōlea. Every post is also a
newsletter issue; the site is where the archive lives and where a reader decides to
subscribe. Success: people finish the piece, read another, and sign up.

## Brand Personality

Candid, precise, unhurried. Confidence without shouting; opinions stated plainly;
room to breathe. The typography does the talking.

## Anti-references

- Ghost / Casper: blue hero, card-grid blog template.
- Medium / Substack: generic centered platform column with a follow button.
- The Kōlea admin's dark "Abyssal" ocean: that is the operator's tool, not the reader's page.

## Design Principles

1. Reading first. Every decision is judged by whether a 1,500-word essay is a pleasure to finish.
2. Two moods, one voice. Folio is the printed book; Signal is the magazine. Neither borrows from the other.
3. Motion with intent. Ambitious choreography on arrival and between pages, then out of the way while reading.
4. No platform smell. Nothing should look like it came from a blogging product's theme gallery.
5. Works without JavaScript. Motion is enhancement; every page reads fully with scripts off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum everywhere. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all choreography,
view transitions and scroll-driven effects. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus.
Pages render completely with JavaScript disabled.
