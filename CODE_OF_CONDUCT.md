# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is smart and acting in good faith. Argue with the
code, not the person who wrote it.

Nobody here is required to know what you know. "You should already understand this" is
not feedback, it's a flex, and it makes the project worse. If someone asks a question
you find obvious, either answer it or scroll past.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Examples

**Contributes to a healthy community:**

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
  from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

**Unacceptable:**

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission
- Condescension dressed up as technical rigor
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Enforcement

This is a small project with one maintainer. Instances of abusive, harassing, or
otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through GitHub's report
feature or by contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be
respected.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.
